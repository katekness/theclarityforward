The Clarity Method

See the full picture

Once the full picture is clear, everything gets sorted into five decisions:

  • Keepwhat’s working.
  • Fixwhat’s creating friction.
  • Focuson what matters most now.
  • Delegatewhat doesn’t need your direct attention.
  • Stopwhat no longer creates enough value.
