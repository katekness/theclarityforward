Privacy policy

Clarity Forward respects your privacy. This policy explains what information I collect through theclarityforward.com, the Clarity Snapshot, Clarity Health Check and Decision Session workspaces, and booked sessions; how I use it; who can see it; and the choices you have. “I” means Kate Kness, doing business as Clarity Forward.

Information I collect

I only collect information you choose to share with me, plus a small amount of basic technical information described below.

  • Contact and messages: your name, email address, phone number, and anything you include when you email, text or message me.
  • Booking a call or session: the details you enter in Cal.com when scheduling, such as your name, email, phone number, business name, and answers to booking questions. Payments are handled by Cal.com and its payment processor; I don’t see or store your card details.
  • The Clarity Snapshot: your answers and results, and the details you choose to provide, such as your name, email, business name and size, role, business stage, priorities, transition plans, which professional advisors you have and whether you’d like introductions, how supported you feel, business details (such as industry, location, revenue range and years in business), notes, phone number, and whether you agree to be contacted about introductions or updates. I also record how you reached the Snapshot (for example, a partner’s link or an event) and anonymous progress through its steps.

Health Check and Decision Session workspaces (invited clients only)

If you’re invited to a Health Check or book a Decision Session, I also collect:

  • Your pre-work: your answers to the intake or pre-session questions, your “last week” list, and any notes you add.
  • Your linked Snapshot: a copy of the Snapshot results you choose to connect, and later a retake if you choose to take one.
  • Workweek Tracker entries: short notes you log during an agreed observation window about what came up in your work, such as repeated tasks, approvals, interruptions and handoffs, with optional time estimates. The tracker records what happens, not how productive anyone is.
  • Messages: your answers to my questions and any notes you add to your plan.
  • Decision Lab entries: the numbers and “what-if” scenarios you choose to try and save, such as hours, costs and changes you’re considering.
  • Plan and progress: plan items, status updates, check-in notes, decisions and introductions we record together.
  • Team members you suggest: their name, email and role (see “Team members” below).
  • Case study information (only if you agree in writing): your sharing choices, starting and follow-up measurements, your reflection, and any quote you approve.
  • My working notes and findings: my private discovery notes, the observations I prepare, and the report I publish to you. My private notes are never shown in your workspace.
  • Conversation notes: notes from our conversations and, only with your permission, recordings and transcripts.

I don’t ask for client records, financial account access or confidential personnel files, and I ask that you not enter your own clients’ or members’ names or confidential details in the workspace.

Basic technical information

To keep the site and workspaces working and secure, I collect limited technical information: anonymous step-by-step progress through the Snapshot, basic error reports when a page fails (with personal details removed), a record of which emails were sent and whether they were delivered, and the security information needed to sign you in. I don’t use advertising trackers.

Cookies and similar storage: workspaces use a secure sign-in cookie that keeps you signed in on that device for up to 30 days. Your browser may also store small preferences, such as whether you prefer the simple or detailed view of the Decision Lab. I don’t use cookies for advertising.

How I use your information

  • To respond to you, schedule and deliver sessions, and send your Snapshot results.
  • To prepare and deliver your Health Check or Decision Session, publish your report, and support you afterwards.
  • To follow up with you about working together and, if you ask, to make introductions to other professionals.
  • To send the emails your workspace relies on (see “Emails you’ll receive”).
  • To understand which partners, events and pages bring people to the Snapshot, and to improve my services.
  • To keep the site and workspaces secure, including limiting repeated sign-in attempts.

I do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Who can see your information

Health Check and Decision Session workspaces are private. You can see your own workspace, and I can see it as the administrator. No other client or visitor can access it. Getting in requires your private invitation link or your email address, plus a one-time code sent to your email.

Team members

If a business owner suggests team members for a Health Check, I review the request and send the invitations myself, and only after the owner has let those people know. Taking part is always optional.

  • What team members do: a short questionnaire about how work flows, with three quick numbers. If the owner chooses, they can also log moments from their own week in the same Workweek Tracker, with an optional daily email reminder they can turn off.
  • What team members see: only their own page and their own answers.
  • What the owner sees: my written summary of themes, and the team’s combined numbers alongside the owner’s own. The owner never sees individual answers or tracker entries. A team member’s name is shown only if that person chooses to share it. Combined numbers appear only once at least two people have answered, and they’re fixed at the time I share them, so one person’s answers can’t be worked out.
  • Team members’ choices: they can ask me to see or delete their information at any time.

Examples and case studies

I never share anything about your organization as an example, testimonial or case study without your written permission. Your signed agreement says exactly what may be shared (for example, your name, business name, approved quotes or before-and-after numbers), and I’ll show you the final wording before anything is published. You can withdraw permission at any time. Team members’ views appear in a case study only as combined, unnamed themes, and only if you’ve agreed.

Emails you’ll receive

Depending on how you use Clarity Forward, you may receive your Snapshot results; invitations and one-time sign-in codes; notices that I’ve sent a question or published your report; plan and check-in updates; a daily tracker reminder (only if you turn it on, or on by default for team members, who can turn it off); a Snapshot retake invitation; a reminder before a Decision Session; and a notice two weeks before your workspace closes. These are part of the service you’ve asked for. You can unsubscribe from follow-up marketing emails at any time.

Services I use

I rely on a small number of trusted services, each of which handles information under its own privacy policy:

  • GitHub Pages: hosts this website.
  • Google Fonts: the fonts on this website, which your browser loads from Google.
  • Lovable and Lovable Cloud: host the Clarity Snapshot and client workspaces, store your answers securely, and send the app’s emails.
  • HubSpot: my customer relationship system. Snapshot contact details, results and follow-up information are copied there so I can keep track of conversations and follow-ups.
  • Cal.com (and its payment processor): scheduling and payments for booked sessions. Decision Session bookings also create your private workspace.
  • Google and IONOS: email.
  • Zoho Sign: signing agreements electronically.
  • AI-assisted tools: transcribing conversations and helping draft notes and reports. I review and finalize everything myself.

Keeping your information

  • Contact, booking and Snapshot information: kept only as long as it’s useful for responding to you, working together, or meeting legal or tax requirements.
  • Workspace access: a Health Check workspace stays open for 90 days after your report is delivered (or 30 days after a scheduled Snapshot retake, if later), and for as long as we keep working together. A Decision Session workspace stays open for 30 days after your recommendations. You’ll get an email two weeks before it closes. Closing access doesn’t delete your information.
  • Health Check and Decision Session information, including recordings: kept for up to 12 months after your report is delivered, unless you ask me to delete it sooner or we agree to keep it longer for ongoing work.
  • Technical logs: error reports and email delivery records are deleted after 90 days.
  • Case study material you’ve approved for publication: kept for as long as your written permission lasts.

How I protect your information

Workspaces are invitation-only and use one-time email codes instead of passwords, with limits on repeated attempts. Your information is stored in a secured database that can’t be read directly from a browser, and access is checked every time. Team members’ answers are kept separate from the owner’s view. No system is perfectly secure, but I take reasonable steps to protect your information and review the app’s security regularly.

Your choices

You can ask me to see, update, export or delete the personal information I have about you at any time, except for anything I’m required to keep by law. You can also choose not to link your Snapshot, skip optional questions, turn off reminders, and unsubscribe from follow-up emails. Email me and I’ll respond promptly.

Children

Clarity Forward is for business and nonprofit leaders and their teams. It isn’t intended for anyone under 18, and I don’t knowingly collect information from children.
