Sound familiar?

The business works. It just still runs through you.

  • Your team asks you the same questions every week.
  • Vacation still means checking email.
  • Work waits until you approve it.
  • Workarounds have quietly become “the way we do it.”

That isn't a work ethic problem. It's a pattern, and patterns can be found.

How it works

Five steps, about 20 minutes

  1. 1

    List what lands on you

    Scroll back through two weeks of your calendar, inbox and texts and write down every time something needed you. Prompts help you catch the small stuff.

  2. 2

    Name the bottleneck type

    Give each item a letter. Your most common one is your main bottleneck type, and the audit tells you what to do about it.

  3. 3

    Score what's costing you most

    Rate each item on how often it comes up, what happens if it waits, and how easy it is to hand off. Your quick wins rise to the top.

  4. 4

    Take the 30-Day Step-Away Test

    Seven areas of your business, from sales to systems. See which would run fine, slow down, or break without you.

  5. 5

    Sort it and plan one handoff

    Keep, Fix, Focus, Delegate or Stop, then choose one thing to hand off and follow a simple week-by-week plan.

Yes. Your answers save to your account, so you can start on your phone and finish on your laptop.

The Snapshot is a 3-minute check on how your business is doing overall. The audit goes deeper and gets specific: it lists exactly what lands on you, scores it, and gives you a plan to hand one thing off.

Does it work for nonprofits?

Yes. Swap "clients" for donors, volunteers or program participants. The patterns are the same.
